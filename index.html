How to Play

🤖 AI Solver Mode

  • Think of a secret 5-letter word.
  • I will make a guess on the board.
  • Use the input box to enter clues: G (Green), Y (Yellow), or B (Gray/Black).

🎮 Play Modes (Daily, Challenge, etc.)

  • Guess the secret word in 6 tries.
  • Just start typing on your physical keyboard or use the on-screen keyboard (no input box needed).
  • Press Enter to submit and Backspace to delete.

⚔️ Challenge a Friend

Enter a secret 5-letter word for your friend (and their AI solver) to guess.
